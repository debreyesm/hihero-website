Worker Questions

Frequently Asked Questions

Am I required to accept every shift?

No. You may review the information provided and accept or decline an available opportunity. Once you accept, HiHero and the client are relying on you to arrive as scheduled.

Does applying guarantee that I will receive work?

No. Applying places you on the HiHero worker list. Opportunities depend on client demand, your experience, location, availability and successful completion of required onboarding.

How will I learn about available shifts?

HiHero may contact qualified workers through WhatsApp, text message, phone or another approved communication method with the role, property area, date, time, pay and instructions.

What should I wear?

The required uniform may vary by assignment. You will receive dress code instructions before the shift. Workers are expected to arrive clean, professional and ready to work.

What happens after I accept a shift?

You will receive confirmation and reporting instructions. Arrive early enough to locate the correct entrance, check in as instructed and be ready to begin at the scheduled start time.
